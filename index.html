📖 Quick Start Guide

Interactive map of Victoria for the Scotchmans Hill sales team.

1. Pick your name

First time you open it, select your name from the dropdown at the top of the sidebar. The map remembers it on your device.

2. Get your bearings
  • Each suburb is shaded by the rep who covers it
  • Hover a suburb to preview it
  • Click a suburb to lock it — full details appear in the panel. Click again to unlock
  • Use the search box to jump to a suburb or customer by name
3. The three tabs
  • View — see who covers a suburb and the customers in it
  • Edit — reassign a suburb or edit customer details (click a suburb first)
  • Leads — your prospect leads. Unclaimed ones sit in the shared pool. Click "Claim" to take one on
4. Finding new venues
  1. Click a suburb to lock it in
  2. Click Find nearby venues
  3. Coloured pins appear — click any for details, phone, website, and a Google Maps link
  4. Use the legend to hide/show venue types
  5. Use the search box to filter by name, type, cuisine or brand
  6. Click Export venues XLSX to download what's visible
5. Marker shapes
  • ◆ Diamond = Lead
  • ▲ Triangle = Prospect
  • ● Circle = Unassigned pool
Tips
If things look broken, try Ctrl+Shift+R (hard refresh).
Your name is saved per device — switch devices and you'll need to pick again.
Venue finder data comes from OpenStreetMap — smaller venues may not always appear.
